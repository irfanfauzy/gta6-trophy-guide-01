    Roadmap to the platinum

    Built from the official descriptions only: finish the story, then the side strands, then activities and robberies, then collectibles and exploration — and 100% completion last. No trophy needs online play, and the game launched with no online mode, so the whole list is single-player. Early estimates put the full run at roughly 200 hours (PowerPyx) to 300+ hours (Push Square); both are guesses from launch week, not measured times.

    Missable trophies

    How this list is built: only one trophy is flagged missable by a published guide — PowerPyx, reported by GGRecon, says a first meditation in Chapter 1 can be missed. Everything else here is flagged from its official description alone, because it is tied to one named mission or one specific moment; no reliable missable list exists yet (the game launched 19 November 2026), and mission replay may recover some of them. The safe play is the same either way: keep a few manual saves before the big missions.

    About & sources

    Where the facts come from

    • Official names, descriptions and grades — the PSN trophy list via Exophase, cross-checked on PSNProfiles. The two agree on every name, description and grade. 55 trophies: 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze; 28 hidden on PlayStation until earned.
    • Counts (250 stunts, 42 shooting-range challenges, 18 meditation spots, 52 areas and the rest) — PSN's progress targets via PSNProfiles.
    • The one confirmed missable — PowerPyx's GTA 6 Trophy Guide, reported by GGRecon: a first meditation in Chapter 1 can be missed.
    • Time estimates — PowerPyx (~200 hours) and Push Square (300+ hours), both early estimates.
    • What 100% completion covers — GTA Culture's completion guide, via Heyday.
    • No online trophies — Push Square and GTABase, via the Exophase list.

    What this guide is honest about

    • Every guide is written in our own words for this entry. No guide text is copied from any other guide; the official names and descriptions are the exception, shown as PlayStation publishes them.
    • Where a guide says how to do something, it is our own advice from the official description — not yet verified in the game. Where a fact is not pinned down (the exact trigger of a vague trophy, the full missable list), the guide says so instead of guessing.
    • No Rockstar logo, artwork, box art or game screenshot is used anywhere in this guide. The look is our own.

    How to use it

    • Tick a trophy when you earn it — progress saves in this browser (localStorage) and survives reloads.
    • Export progress downloads your ticks as a JSON file; Import progress reads one back.
    • Hide story spoilers hides the names and descriptions of hidden story trophies until you earn them.
    • Filters: kind (story, missable, collectibles, activities, online), grade, earned or not, and search.
    • This app is static files and works with the network off. It fetches only its own files — the trophy list in data/trophies.json — and nothing else.

    Cheats warning

    Turning on a cheat disables trophies, according to PowerPyx — and you cannot save once one is used, per GameSpot and PC Gamer. Do not use cheats on a platinum run.
